About

One toolkit, from theorems to the brain.

I'm a control engineer and computational neuroscientist. My PhD was on the stability of positive and monotone dynamical systems — proving when large, coupled nonlinear systems settle rather than spiral out — and that question has followed me across fields ever since.

I've used it to optimize wind farms, study delay-driven instabilities in power grids, model how epidemics spread, and — at the Max Planck Institute — probe how the brain perceives and learns with eye-tracking, EEG, and MRI. The common thread is feedback under uncertainty, which is exactly the problem an autonomous vehicle has to solve. That's what I work on now.
